Our Standards and Quality

Because we care about every step of production

Warehouse

We manage product storage systematically and hygienically, separating products by type. Our facility meets international standards and is located in Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya province on an area of 14 rai, ensuring safety and readiness to meet customer demands.

Production Capabilities

Greatest Pet Care Co., Ltd. is a specialized pet food manufacturer and exporter with over 31,000 tons of annual production capacity. Equipped with modern machinery and strict quality control, certified by international standards such as ISO 9001:2015, HACCP, GMP, Halal, and BRC.

Safety & Quality You Can Trust

Every ingredient is carefully selected and processed under strict quality control. We ensure hygienic storage and production with international certifications including ISO, HACCP, GMP, Halal, and BRC — supervised by experienced food science professionals.

Product Development
