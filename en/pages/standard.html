Our Standards Explained

Click each standard to learn more about our commitments.

HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point)

HACCP is a system used to identify, evaluate, and control potential hazards throughout the production process. It is widely recognized across countries for ensuring consumer product safety.

ISO 9001:2015

ISO 9001:2015 is a quality management system that certifies an organization’s process control and quality assurance, driving continual improvement and customer satisfaction.

AAFCO (The Association of American Feed Control Officials)

Our production aligns with AAFCO standards. Our products meet complete-and-balanced nutrient profiles based on the U.S. National Research Council (NRC) and use high-quality ingredients with modern manufacturing.

GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice)

GMP sets the minimum requirements to ensure products are safe, suitable for consumption, and quality-controlled at every stage of production.

HALAL SUCI

Our products are mixed, seasoned, assembled, and packaged in accordance with Islamic principles, certified by the Islamic Food Research Centre (Hong Kong), ensuring compliance, cleanliness, and safety under Islamic law.

BRC

The BRC (British Retail Consortium) Global Standard focuses on product safety, quality, and legality—assuring consumers that products are high-quality, safe, and compliant with regulations.
